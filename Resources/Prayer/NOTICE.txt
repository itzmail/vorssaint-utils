Prayer times: what this folder holds and where it comes from

The code that works out the times (Services/Prayer) is Vorssaint's, under
GPL-3.0-or-later, ported from the pieces below. The files here keep their own
terms.

places.json
  Every location in Kemenag RI's (Bimas Islam) prayer schedule, named as
  Kemenag writes them, each with a reference point fitted to Kemenag's
  published 2026 schedule; plus Makkah, Madinah and Jeddah (coordinates from
  en.wikipedia.org). From Oyen Poni (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Jatevo
  Labs), the Rust app whose prayer engine Vorssaint's is a port of. Kemenag's
  own tables are not included.

adzan.m4a
  "Maghrib Adhan at the Masjid al Haram, Mecca - 25 Feb, 2012" by
  3omar Faruq (YouTube: yodasdad), from Wikimedia Commons:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maghrib_Adhan_at_the_Masjid_al_Haram,_Mecca_-_25_Feb,_2012.webm
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Changes: only the adzan kept (1:54 to 4:52 of the video), made mono,
  faded in and out, its loudness levelled, and saved as AAC (80 kbit/s).

Adhan (adhan-js 4.4.6)
  The sun's position and the moments behind prayer times
  (Services/Prayer/PrayerAstronomy.swift is a Swift port of the parts used,
  by way of Oyen Poni's Rust port). Formulas are from Jean Meeus,
  "Astronomical Algorithms".
  Source: https://github.com/batoulapps/adhan-js

The prayer times are checked in Tests/PrayerEngineTests.swift against
Kemenag's published 2026 schedule (via api.myquran.com) and against the
reference engine. The schedules are test data only.
